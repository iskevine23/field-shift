Method and limits

Data: NASA POWER monthly values (satellite- and model-derived, gridded). For each year, the chosen months are averaged, giving about 25 yearly values. Tests: Mann-Kendall trend test (normal approximation, tie-corrected) and Sen's slope. Multiple tests: Benjamini-Hochberg correction in the two tables.

Limits: about 25 points is short for climate trends; year-to-year memory (autocorrelation) is not corrected, which can make trends look more significant than they are; the data is gridded and model-based, so it describes a wide area; "significant" does not mean "important". Season groups are a simple choice for Rwanda. Only NASA POWER variables are included; no satellite image layers yet.
